PRIVACY POLICY

Daily Dharma · Last updated October 9, 2026

Daily Dharma is a free, unofficial LOST fan trivia game. This page explains what information the game collects and how it is used.

Playing without an account

You can play without logging in. Your answers, scores and streaks are then saved only in your own browser on your device. Nothing about your games is sent to us.

What we collect if you log in

  • Your email address, used to log you in. If you log in with Google or Apple, we receive the email address and basic profile details (such as your name) that you allow that service to share.
  • The username you choose. Other logged-in players may be able to see it.
  • Your game results: the date of each game, the answers you picked, your score, and whether you finished.
  • Friends. If you accept a friend request, that friend can see your game results and stats. Removing the friend stops this.

How we use it

Only to run the game: logging you in, saving your progress, keeping your stats and streaks in sync across your devices, and showing your username. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the services listed below that run the game.

Services that run the game

  • Supabase stores accounts and game results.
  • Google and Apple handle login when you choose those options.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver the font and the login library. Like any website, they see your device's IP address when they send these files.

The game does not use analytics, tracking or advertising cookies.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and everything saved with it, email dailydharma42@gmail.com from the address you log in with, and it will be removed. Logging out clears the game history saved in that browser.

Children

Daily Dharma is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions: dailydharma42@gmail.com

Daily Dharma is a fan project and is not affiliated with ABC, Disney or the creators of LOST.
